I’m Adnan Khan Adib, a student and community initiative builder from Bangladesh. My interests include education, youth engagement, social impact, communication and practical uses of technology.

Through volunteer-led initiatives, I work to help students access learning resources, guidance and opportunities. I’m interested in building projects that are useful, transparent and sustainable—not just visible.

Outside project work, I enjoy exploring new ideas, learning, and finding better ways to turn plans into practical outcomes.
